# Combat

During their turn Warriors may chose initiate combat.

Warriors may attack dwarfs on the same tile as them, or a stronghold on an adjacent tile.

Combat is a simple rolling of dice.

Workers always roll a gold dice for attacking or defending. (They will get to roll more dice at a higher level)

Warriors will roll different dice depending on their level.

The team with the highest sum on the rolled dice wins.  

If the battle is a draw then the attacking team gets displaced to an adjecent tile of the defending team's choice.

All participants of the losing team get sent to the hospital.  

## Hospital

Dwarfs that lost a battle are sent here. At the start of a players turn place all your dwarfs that were in the hospital adjacent to your stronghold laying down. These dwarfs may not take a turn.
You may stand up all dwarfs that were laying down from the previous turn and use them normally.  
This effectively removes dwarfs that lost a battle from 1 turn of play.  

## Conscription

Starting at stronghold level 2 warriors may call on the aid of worker dwarfs to join them in their conquests.  
These dwarf must be adjacent to the warrior dwarf to be conscripted. All conscripted dwarfs get to add their dice roll to the attack.  
Conscripted dwarfs don't need to move.  
If they lose all conscripted dwarfs and the warrior are sent to the hospital.
