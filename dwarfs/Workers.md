# Worker Dwarfs

Workers are your primary dwarf. They get stuff done.  
Their main tasks are building mining and upgrading.

|2|5|1|May Build 2 rail roads for their action|

|4|9|2|Rail Roads don't cost stone or copper |

## Actions

- [Explore](#explore)
- [Build/Upgrade](#build--upgrade)
- [Mine](#mine)

### Explore

If the Dwarf is adjacent to an unexplored tile you may use the explore action.  
Remove the adjacent mountain tile and check underneath. If you find a resource place a corresponding tile onto the spot you explored to denote a resource vein.  

Roll one stone die + 1 die for the resource you discovered. (If you discovered stone you get to roll 2 stone dice.)

Additionally you get Gems for discovering resources

- stone = 1 gem
- copper = 2 gems
- gold = 3 gems

### Build / Upgrade

If the Dwarf is adjacent to the tile you want to build on or upgrade on you may use your action to pay the resource cost of the building/upgrade

They may build/upgrade the following  

- [RailRoad](../buildings/RailRoad.md)
- [Mine](../buildings/Mine.md)  
- [Stronghold](../buildings/Stronghold.md)  
- [Monument](../buildings/Monument.md)

### Mine

If the dwarf is standing on a mine or resource tile they may chose to you their action to mine it.

Roll 1 dice for the resource tile.  

If the resource tile has a mine on it you may use the mine provided you have the owner's permission.  (They have to grant it to you)
