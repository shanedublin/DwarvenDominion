# Warrior Dwarves 

Warriors operate a little differently than workers.  
They are a little slower and can't use mine tracks.  
They also have passive actions.  

---

|Level|Movement|Dice|Bonus|
|-----|----|----|----|
|1|3|Iron Dice||
|2|4|Stone Dice|Conscription|
|3|5|Stone and Gold Dice|May sacrifice attack for 3 more movement|
|4|6|Iron stone and gold Dice|May sacrifice movement for additional action|

---
Conscription: May force friendly dwarfs to fight alongside warrior, adding their dice to his.

## Actions

### Destroy Track

While on top of a track you may use your action to destroy it.  

### Attack Dwarf

Attacks all enemy dwarfs on current tile. Roll dice based on dwarf level.  
Sum all dice for each team. The losing team's dwarfs get send back to the hospital. Ties end with the attacking team being forced to an adjacent tile of the defending teams choice.  See combat for more details

### Attack Stronghold

A warrior may attack an enemy stronghold for the chance of stealing gems.  

Normal combat rules apply

If the warrior wins, roll the gold dice. You may steal that number of gems from player.  
If the warrior loses, Send the warrior and all helping dwarfs to the hospital.  

## Passive Actions

Passive actions are all optional and can be changed whenever the player decides.  (This might be a good time for bribery)  
These actions are not default, meaning the player owning the warrior must declare during the players turn that they can't take an action. (Warriors be sleeping on the job)

### Blocking Movement

Warriors may block the movement of other workers (Not warriors)  

### Blocking Building

Warriors map prevent other players from building on the tile they are standing on

### Blocking Mining

Warriors stationed at a mine may prevent workers from mining and from players getting their resources at the start of their turn.
