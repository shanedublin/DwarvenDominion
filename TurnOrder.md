# Determining Turn Order

Everyone rolls one of each dice. (Stone, copper, and Gold)  
The person with the lowest roll goes first.  
Add these to your resource totals.  
Player turn proceeds with the next lowest player going next. Gold is worth the most, copper is worth 2nd, and stone is worth the least.

## Examples

Player 1 rolls: 2 stone, 3 copper and 1 gold  
Player 2 rolls: 8 stone, 3 copper and 1 gold  

Player 1 would go first.

Player 1 rolls 2 stone, 0 copper and 3 gold
Player 2 rolls 8 stone, 5 copper and 0 gold

Player 2 would go first.
