# Trading

There are 2 ways to trade.  
You may trade with players or you may make an exchange with the resource Bank.  
You can make trades at anytime during the game. (Even when it is not your turn)

## Resource Bank

Trade rates with resources bank:  
Stone for copper 5:1  
Copper for gold  10:1  
Gold for gems   10:1  

Note: You may only trade in 1 direction with the resource bank. IE: you can trade 50 stone for 1 gold, but you can't trade 1 gem for 10 gold.

## Player Trading

Players can trade at any time with each other. For example this can be for things like: acquiring missing resources, paying to use a mine, paying off a hit, or setting up a bounty on another player.
