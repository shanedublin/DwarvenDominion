# Taking a Turn

## Collect your resources

You get resources each turn from your stronghold and any mines you own.  
At the start of the game you'll only have a level one stronghold and therefore will only get to roll a single gold dice.  

You get dice based on the level of your mines and the resource type.

Roll your dice and add your resources to your mine-cart.  

## Using Dwarfs

Each dwarf you own on the board gets to take a turn.  

A turn consists of moving then performing an action.  
These are each optional however, movement must occur before taking an action.  

### [Worker](dwarfs/Workers.md)

They mine build and upgrade things!

### [Warrior](dwarfs/Warrior.md)
